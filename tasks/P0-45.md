# P0-45 — Preview page: project areas, their tasks and purpose

> Static design preview (`apps/web/preview/`). Owner note (2026-10-27): the live preview is not
> bound to the usual brief budgets.

## Goal

Owner request of 2026-10-29: "mache mir auf der preview seite eine schöne übersichtliche liste mit
den bereichen und deren aufgaben und wofür". It follows the categorised overview the owner asked
for earlier, which is meant for distributing work later: who owns server, auth and database, who
owns the web app, and who owns the engine.

A new section **"13 — Project areas"** follows section 12 (design rules) and contains:

1. **Intro and legend.** One sentence on what the list is for, the date of the status, and a legend
   with three states: done, in progress, planned. Each state is shown as a dot plus a word, never
   by colour alone. The colours are `--accent` for done, `--tone-b` for in progress and `--muted`
   for planned.
2. **Area cards.** There are nine cards in a responsive grid: one or two columns on desktop, one on
   mobile. The areas are engine, web app, design system, data model, server, database and storage,
   realtime, tools and quality, and operations and release. Each card shows:
   - a number and title, plus one sentence on what the area is for ("Wofür");
   - the owner role and the main folders (`code`);
   - the area's parts as a list. Each part has a short name, one simple sentence on what it does,
     and a status. The parts are the same as the categorised overview: for example the engine's
     C++ core, the WebGL2/WebGPU drawing, the TypeScript bridge, the logic layer, the CMake and
     Emscripten build, the tests and the playground;
   - a "Connected to" line naming the areas it uses or serves.
3. **Who does what.** A compact table maps the eight roles to their areas and folders. On narrow
   screens it scrolls inside its own container.
4. **Two clarifications** in one quiet note. React 19, Vite and the own editor belong to the web
   app, not the engine. Playwright is not a dependency: only the Chrome for Testing binary is used,
   and the e2e suite comes with P6-09.

The statuses come from `board.json` on 2026-10-29. Every text lives in `en.json` and `de.json`
(identical keys, du-form, `ss`), with short, simple sentences. The section works in the dark and
light themes (P0-42) and has no horizontal overflow at 390 px. The page's contents, if it lists
sections, gains the new entry.

**Why:** the owner wants one place where the whole team sees what exists, what each part is for
and who will own it.

**Satisfies:** D3

one-sentence explanations and status, and a "connected to" line, plus the roles table and the note.

in dark and light; nothing overflows at 390 px.
A3 WHEN the gates run, THEN `board-check`, `design-check`, `changelog-entry` pass with records,
language-check is OK, en/de keys are identical, masthead P0-45, screenshots and log are committed.
