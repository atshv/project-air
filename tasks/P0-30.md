# P0-30 — Flexible zone layout, less magnetic drag and smaller controls

> **Size:** 400–900 words. UI interaction in the static design preview (`apps/web/preview/`).

## Goal

1. The two zone items sit **side by side or stacked** depending on how they are dragged/placed and
   can be **individually reordered/swapped** inside the zone.
2. Dragging in/out is **less magnetic**: 1:1 pointer tracking, no mid-drag snap, clean
   combine/separate animations.
3. The **+/− and fit buttons** are visually smaller, with accessible hit areas.

**Why:** placement-driven layout, individual reordering, less magnetism, smaller controls.

**Satisfies:** D3

## Acceptance

A1 WHEN the items are grouped, THEN the zone picks horizontal or vertical from the placement
(side-by-side or stacked slots), animates its resize and stays clamped inside the surface.
A2 WHEN a docked item is dragged within the zone, THEN it can reorder/swap with the other item and
switch orientation (above/below vs left/right) with a clean settle. 
A3 WHEN dragging in/out or onto the other free item, THEN the item tracks the pointer 1:1 (no
mid-drag snap/dock/group), a subtle drop hint appears, and docking/grouping commits only on release

`prefers-reduced-motion` instant; no edge created.

areas stay ≥32 px, labels/focus are unchanged and there is no overlap at 1440×900 or 390×844;
`board-check`, `design-check`, `changelog-entry` pass with records committed; screenshots + log

## Specifications

- Slots from item sizes + padding 16 + gap 12; orientation state; zone bounds = slots + padding,
  clamped to surface (below toolbar on desktop).
- Orientation: dragged item's centre offset from the other item; vertical dominance → stacked,
  horizontal → side by side; inside-zone drags use drop position relative to the other centre;
  hysteresis avoids flicker.
- Drop hint: subtle outline/tint on the target, no layout change.
- Commit on release: release position decides (documented margins); outside stays free, inside
  animates to a slot. Dragged items track the pointer exactly, clamped only at the canvas edge.
- Motion: transform/opacity only, spring ease, reduced motion instant, tokens only.
- Smaller controls: 28 px visual buttons, 32 px hit area, 12 px glyphs, ~32 px pill; labels/focus
  unchanged.

## Pitfalls

- Grouping must be a release commit (P0-29 grouped mid-drag); keep the drop anchor so the animation
  is not jumpy. Use a dominance ratio/hysteresis against diagonal flapping.
- Keep the P0-29 collapse (hidden/inert after both leave) and resize re-fit.
- `design-check` rejects raw colours/off-grid spacing/numeric z-index; `language-check` German in
  code; `board-check` before any `pnpm`; `.changes` mtime = masthead time.

## Edge cases

- Group vertically then resize to 390 → still inside/visible; horizontal may fall back to vertical
  on narrow widths.
- Drop exactly on the midpoint; drop outside both; cancel/second pointer; locale switch mid-drag;
  reduced motion; zoom 0.5/1.5.
