# P0-39 — Live demo: quiet resize frame, done items dimmed, design rules page

> Static design preview (`apps/web/preview/`) plus the two spec lines it changes.
> Owner note (2026-10-27): the live preview is not bound to the usual brief budgets.

## Goal

Three owner requests of 2026-10-28:

1. **Resize without the eight dots.** "verbessere das vergrösserungs design, mache es ohne diese 8
   punkte an den seiten". The selection keeps its 1 px `--accent` frame, but the eight 8 px square
   handles go away. The frame itself is the handle: each edge has an invisible hit band 8 screen px
   wide, centred on the line, and each corner an invisible 16 × 16 px hit square. The cursor names
   the direction (`ns-resize`, `ew-resize`, `nwse-resize`, `nesw-resize`). When the pointer is over
   an edge, that edge shows as a 2 px accent line (`--outline-width`). Over a corner, a short
   2 px accent corner bracket shows (16 px arms). Both fade in at `--motion-hover`, and stay while
   the resize runs. At rest nothing but the frame is visible. Hit areas and lines keep their screen
   size at every zoom. The behaviour (anchor at the opposite edge, minimums, content height, photo
   adoption, undo) is unchanged. The section 10 chrome specimen shows the new frame with one hovered
   edge and one corner bracket instead of eight squares.
2. **Done checklist items dimmed.** "in der checkliste werden die erledigten punkte dunkel
   dargestellt und nicht durchstrichen". A checked item's text turns `--muted` and is no longer
   struck through. The tick and the counter stay. Design law §6 gets a "Done (checklist entry)" row:
   text `--muted`, never struck through.
3. **Every design rule on the page.** "führe auch alle Designregeln auf der preview seite auf". A
   new section "12 — Design rules" lists every rule of `docs/design-rules.md`, §0 to §14, one card
   per section with its rules as short lines. MUST and NEVER rules are marked, and each card names
   the section where the page shows the rule at work. The text comes from `en.json` and `de.json`
   (identical keys, du-form, `ss`). It is a faithful condensation; where a rule carries numbers
   (spacing series, motion times, layer order, distances), the numbers appear unchanged.

The specs follow the demo. `docs/canvas-acceptance.md` L14 says the frame's edges and corners are the
handles: hit band of at least 8 px, the hovered edge or corner lit in accent, and no square
handles. The engine still draws the P4-17 handles, so aligning it is a separate engine task. This
brief does not change the engine.

**Why:** the owner judges the product by the live demo; the eight squares read as noise on calm
cards, strikethrough makes done items harder to read, and the rules belong next to their tokens.

**Satisfies:** D3

## Acceptance

A1 WHEN a single object is selected, THEN only the 1 px frame shows; hovering an edge or corner lights
that edge (2 px) or corner bracket, the cursor names the direction, and dragging there resizes as
before against the opposite edge or corner.
A2 WHEN a checklist item is ticked, THEN its text is `--muted` without strikethrough and the counter
updates; design law §6 states the rule.
A3 WHEN section 12 is opened in EN and DE, THEN it lists every section §0–§14 of the design law with
its rules, and nothing overflows at 390 px.
A4 WHEN the gates run, THEN `board-check`, `design-check`, `changelog-entry` pass with records,
language-check is OK, en/de keys are identical, masthead P0-39, screenshots and log are committed.
