# P4-24 — Trackpad and wheel defaults (L15, desktop part)

## Goal

Two fingers pan, pinch zooms around the finger centre and `Cmd/Ctrl`+wheel zooms; wheel/pointer
normalisation lives in one input module with unit tests and never moves the view during an object
drag. Touch and the manual tablet check stay in hardening.

**Why:** owner decision `Q16` — two fingers pan and pinch zooms, `Cmd/Ctrl`+wheel zooms; L15 desktop
part, so the canvas feels natural on a trackpad and never mixes moving an object with moving the view.

**Satisfies:** D10

## Scope

- `packages/engine/` wheel/pointer normalisation in one input-layer function: plain two-finger
  deltaX/deltaY pans 1:1, `Cmd/Ctrl`+wheel zooms around the pointer world point, two-pointer pinch
  zooms around the midpoint; zoom clamps to 0.1 … 8. Suppressed targets (field/scrollable, P4-14)
  keep their own wheel. While a drag is active wheel/pinch do not move the view; `Cmd/Ctrl` is
  `metaKey` on macOS and `ctrlKey` elsewhere.
- `packages/engine/` no second path: the existing pointer pan/zoom (P4-14) and this normalisation
  share one camera update; no key or wheel handler exists outside the input layer; the engine core
  camera API stays the only view state.
- `apps/engine-playground/`: a record proving two-finger pan (camera moves, zoom unchanged),
  `Cmd/Ctrl`+wheel zoom around the pointer (anchor stable within a documented tolerance), pinch
  around the midpoint, wheel over a field not moving the canvas, and no view move during a drag;
  capture record under `reviews/evidence/` (1440×900, 390×844). If CDP wheel dispatch hangs (as in
  P4-23), dispatch in-page and record that explicitly, never claim a CDP pass.
- Tests: normalisation mapping (plain wheel → pan sign/scale, modified wheel → zoom anchor, pinch →
  midpoint, suppressed → none, drag → no intent), anchor math, clamping at both limits, meta/ctrl per
  platform, no camera update during a drag; `cpp-unit` regression.
  `.changes/P4-24-<short-name>.md` (area `engine`, kind `minor`); `docs/engine.md` documents
  normalisation, anchors, clamp, drag guard and the touch/hardening boundary.

## Out of scope

- Touch gestures, the manual touch-tablet check (hardening, per `Q20`), momentum/inertia for
  fling-pan, scroll-to-zoom as a default (owner decision `Q16` keeps `Cmd/Ctrl`+wheel), new visual
  baselines, contract/database changes, new dependencies, changed or deleted tests.

## Specifications

- One normalisation function, one camera update: no handler outside the input layer; the engine core
  camera stays the only view state (AD-2).
- Values only from tokens where applicable; the wheel scale is a documented interaction factor, the
  camera range stays 0.1 … 8 and the pinch anchor is the midpoint of the two pointers.
- Zoom around a screen point keeps that point's world position stable within a documented tolerance;
  test the anchor math at several zoom levels.
- Suppressed targets keep their own wheel (P4-14 policy); no `preventDefault` on their events.
- While a drag is active, the view does not move at all; after release the normal behaviour resumes.
- No new token, dependency or contract change; touch stays out of scope (hardening).

## Pitfalls

- Two-finger scroll arrives as small wheel deltas; do not multiply the pan scale twice.
- `metaKey` vs `ctrlKey` differ per platform; test both.
- Pinch uses the midpoint anchor and relative scale, not per-pointer drift.
- `preventDefault` on every wheel breaks field scrolling; check suppression first.
- A drag guard that forgets `pointercancel` locks the view after a cancelled drag.
