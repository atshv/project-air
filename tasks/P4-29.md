## Goal

Zones in the engine behave like the live demo:

1. **Arrangement** — members are laid out stacked or side by side (item sizes + 16 px padding +
   12 px gap below a 40 px header); orientation follows the placement (size-normalised offsets,
   1.25 dominance, half-item overlap hold), order follows the centre with a 12 px band.
2. **Release preview** — while a drag is active nothing is committed: a dashed landing slot, the
   other member making room, the frame shrinking around the remaining member when one is pulled out
   (ghost and emptying frames come with P4-30). Enter = pointer over the frame or 25 % item coverage; stay = 24 px catch margin;
   placement decided against drag-start positions.
3. **Release commits exactly one operation** (with inverse, through the P4-05 seam): dock, reorder,
   orientation change, detach.
4. **Motion** — 1:1 drag with lift; every settle 300 ms `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` without
   overshoot; frame resize without layout stretching; reduced motion instant.
