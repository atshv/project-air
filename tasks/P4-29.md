# P4-29 — Zone interaction parity with the live demo (L08)

> Owner decision (2026-10-27): "The engine must work the way the live demo shows it." The reference
> behaviour is the static design preview `apps/web/preview/` (P0-28 … P0-32): read its `README.md`
> sections "Zone interaction" and "Zone name" and `preview.js` (zone section) as the specification.
> This brief is a placeholder for the JIT brief; refine it when P4-27, P4-23 and P4-10 are done.

## Goal

Zones in the engine behave like the live demo:

1. **Arrangement** — members are laid out stacked or side by side (item sizes + 16 px padding +
   12 px gap below a 40 px header); orientation follows the placement (size-normalised offsets,
   1.25 dominance, half-item overlap hold), order follows the centre with a 12 px band.
2. **Release preview** — while a drag is active nothing is committed: a dashed landing slot, the
   other member making room, the frame shrinking around the remaining member when one is pulled out,
   a dimmed dashed frame when the last member leaves, a dashed ghost frame when two free objects
   would group. Enter = pointer over the frame or 25 % item coverage; stay = 24 px catch margin;
   placement decided against drag-start positions.
3. **Release commits exactly one operation** (with inverse, through the P4-05 seam): dock, reorder,
   orientation change, detach, group into a new zone, remove an emptied zone.
4. **Names** — every zone shows its `title` in the header; inline rename through the P4-23 overlay
   (click selects all, Enter/blur commits, Escape restores, empty → default, 40 characters); a new
   zone from grouping is titled "New zone"/"Neue Zone".
5. **Motion** — 1:1 drag with lift; every settle 300 ms `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` without
   overshoot; frame resize without layout stretching; reduced motion instant.

## Decisions to record (ADR-0010 amendment, owner approval given 2026-10-27)

- Auto-arranged members (stored positions written by the release operation) instead of free
  positions inside a zone.
- An emptied zone is removed (one undoable operation) instead of staying empty; "keep content"
  deletion stays for explicit deletes.
- Dropping a free object onto another free object creates a zone containing both (one grouped
  operation); confirm whether the contract needs a group/batch operation (would be contract 0.3.0).

## Acceptance (to refine in the JIT brief)

A1 WHEN members are dragged in, out, reordered or switched between stacked and side by side, THEN the
engine shows the same preview states and commits the same outcomes as the live demo, each as one
operation with an inverse; native tests cover each case.
A2 WHEN the last member leaves or two free objects are grouped, THEN the zone is removed or created as
one undoable operation, and the new zone carries the default title.
A3 WHEN a zone title is edited, THEN the overlay rename rules of the live demo hold and the title is
stored through the contract.
A4 WHEN the task is done, THEN playground side-by-side screenshots against the live demo, `visual`
references, `board-check`, `boundaries`, `cpp-unit`, `visual` and `typecheck` pass with records.
