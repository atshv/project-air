# P4-30 — Zone parity with the live demo II: lifecycle and names (L08)

> Owner decision (2026-10-27): "The engine must work the way the live demo shows it." Reference:
> `apps/web/preview/` (README "Zone interaction" and "Zone name", `preview.js` zone section).
> Placeholder for the JIT brief; refine it when P4-29 and P4-23 are done.

## Goal

1. **Group** — dropping a free object onto another free object (20 % overlap or pointer within
   16 px) shows a dashed ghost frame around the object that stays and, on release, creates a zone
   with both as members, titled "New zone"/"Neue Zone".
2. **Empty zone** — pulling the last member out dims the frame (dashed); the release removes the
   zone; both are single undoable operations.
3. **Names** — every zone shows its contract `title` in a 40 px header (one line, ellipsis); inline
   rename through the P4-23 overlay: click selects all, Enter/blur commits, Escape restores, empty
   returns to the default, 40 characters; the header never stretches during a frame resize.

**Why:** owner decision 2026-10-27: the engine must work the way the live demo shows it.

**Satisfies:** D10

## Decisions to record (ADR-0010 amendment, owner approval given 2026-10-27)

- An emptied zone is removed instead of staying empty; explicit deletion keeps "keep content".
- Dropping a free object onto another creates a zone containing both as one grouped operation;
  confirm whether the contract needs a group/batch operation (then contract 0.3.0 as its own task).

## Acceptance (to refine in the JIT brief)

A1 WHEN two free objects are grouped or the last member leaves, THEN the zone is created or removed
as one undoable operation with the same preview as the live demo; native tests cover both.
A2 WHEN a zone title is edited, THEN the live demo's rename rules hold and the title is stored
through the contract; a new zone carries the default title.
A3 WHEN the task is done, THEN ADR-0010 is amended, playground screenshots match the live demo, and
`board-check`, `boundaries`, `cpp-unit`, `visual` and `typecheck` pass with records.

## Edge cases

- Grouping when one of the two objects is the only member of an existing zone: the existing zone
  wraps both and keeps its title (no new zone, no rename).
- Cancel (Escape, `pointercancel`, lost capture) during a ghost or emptying preview restores the
  committed state without any operation.
- Undo of a group restores both free objects at their pre-drop positions and removes the zone; undo of
  an empty-zone removal restores the zone with its title and the member back in its slot.
- A title edit while the zone is being removed or regrouped is committed first or discarded, never
  applied to the wrong zone.
- Default titles follow the language; a typed title stays as typed across reloads.

## Pitfalls

- The ghost frame and the landing slot are previews only: they must never create a zone or write a
  position before the release.
- The header text must not be scaled with the frame during a resize animation.

## Limits

- **Scope:** only `packages/engine/`, `apps/engine-playground/`, `docs/adr/`, `docs/engine.md`, `.changes/`
- A contract change only as its own task if a group operation is needed.
