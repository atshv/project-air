# P0-24 — Curved edge, visible anchors and zoom controls in the demo

## Goal

The live demo feels like the intended canvas: the connection is a smooth curved path (not a stiff
straight line), the four anchors per card are clearly visible, and the demo has working
`+`, `−` and `Alles anzeigen` controls in the canvas-chrome position.

**Why:** the owner sees the static sample's curved connection and wants the live demo to feel the
same; the anchors are not visible enough and the zoom controls are missing.

**Satisfies:** D3

## Scope

- `apps/web/preview/` (all files):
  1. **Curved edge:** replace the straight SVG line with a cubic Bézier path whose control points
     follow the side of the used anchors (outward normal, e.g. 48 px), so the edge moves smoothly
     while dragging and keeps the `--tone-d` / 1.5-px style. Keep a sensible minimum length when
     cards overlap.
  2. **Visible anchors:** idle anchors clearly visible: 8 px dots, `--fg` fill with a `--surface`
     ring; the used anchors stay `--tone-d`; keep them decorative (`aria-hidden`) and outside the
     drag hit area. No new tokens, no raw colours.
  3. **Zoom controls:** a floating control in the canvas-chrome position (bottom left, 12 px from
     the demo surface edge, design rule §9) with three 36-px buttons: `+`, `−`, `Alles anzeigen`
     (fit). Draw the glyphs with CSS (lines/border shapes) instead of Unicode icons or new
     ui-kit icons; give each button an English `aria-label` and a tooltip via `data-label`/the
     existing preview tooltip pattern. Implement zoom on a world layer (transform scale, e.g.
     0.5–1.5 steps), keep the edge/anchors correct under zoom, and let fit compute the scale and
     translation that shows both cards (also after dragging). Drag math must stay in world
     coordinates; keyboard 16-px nudge stays in world coordinates.
  4. Keep drag threshold, pointer capture, clamping, `Home`, reduced motion, `--tone-d` edge and
     the mobile layout (390×844) intact; no horizontal overflow.
  5. Update the demo hint/legend (curved edge, visible anchors, fit/zoom) and
     `apps/web/preview/README.md`: demo controls are preview-only CSS glyphs; the product's zoom
     control will use ui-kit icons in the engine stage.
  6. Update the masthead meta line (P0-22/P0-23 rule): `Last change: P0-24 — Curved edge, visible
     anchors and zoom controls in the demo` plus the current date/time (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`,
     Europe/Zurich).
- `.changes/P0-24-demo-zoom.md` (area `web`, kind `patch`, one English line).
- Evidence: refreshed `reviews/evidence/P0-24-demo.png` (1440×900, curved edge, visible anchors,
  zoom controls) and `reviews/evidence/P0-24-preview.log` with serve/screenshot and probe results
  (zoom in/out, fit, edge curve under drag/zoom, anchor visibility); keep older screenshots.

## Specifications

- **Curve:** `M x1 y1 C c1x c1y, c2x c2y, x2 y2`; control offsets along the anchor side normal
  (`top` → up, `right` → right, `bottom` → down, `left` → left), both endpoints; recompute in the
  same frame as card movement; the SVG lives inside the zoomed world layer so it scales with the
  cards.
- **Anchors:** 8 px, `--fg` fill, `--surface` ring; used `--tone-d`; `pointer-events: none`;
  positioned at side midpoints in world coordinates.
- **Zoom:** world scale in `[0.5, 1.5]` (steps 0.1 or 0.25); fit = compute card bounds, choose scale
  `min(1, available/needed)`, centre; zoom buttons disabled at limits (`disabled` state from the
  design rules).
- **Controls:** bottom-left inside the demo surface, 12 px from its edge; three 36-px buttons with
  20-px CSS glyphs; focus ring, hover, pressed and disabled states from the design rules; tooltip
  text (English) after 300 ms or on focus.

## Pitfalls

- Do not let the controls capture card drags; they are siblings above the world layer.
- Do not break pointer coordinates under zoom (divide screen deltas by the current scale).
- `check.py` scans `node_modules/`; run board-check before any install.

## Edge cases

- Cards overlapping or touching → curve still valid, minimum visible control offset.
- Mobile: controls remain inside the surface, no overflow.
