# P0-48 — Live demo: visible selection frame, delete that works

> Static design preview (`apps/web/preview/`). Owner note (2026-10-27): the live preview is not
> bound to the usual brief budgets.

## Goal

Owner feedback of 2026-10-29: "Grössenrahmen leicht dicker machen, damit man diese sieht. select
funktioniert nicht, selected delete auch nicht gesehen".

A headless check of main showed three causes:

- The selection frame is 1 px wide and sits exactly on the object's own accent border, so a
  selected card looks almost unchanged.
- Deleting sets the `hidden` attribute, but the objects' own `display` rules override it. The
  status line says "1 deleted" while the card stays on the canvas.
- The count bar with its delete button only appears for two or more selected objects. A single
  selection has no visible way to delete it.

The task fixes all three:

1. **Visible frame.** The single-selection frame and the multi-selection frames are 2 px
   (`--outline-width`) wide and sit 4 px (`--space-4`) outside the object. Their radius is the
   object's radius plus that gap, so the frame stays concentric with the rounded corner. Line,
   gap and hit areas keep their screen size at every zoom. The resize hit bands and corner
   squares move with the frame, centred on its line. The hovered edge lights up 3 px (outline plus
   border width), and the corner arc follows the larger radius. L14 in `docs/canvas-acceptance.md`
   names the new numbers.
2. **Delete removes.** Any demo object marked `hidden` (note cards, blocks, zone items) is really
   not displayed: an explicit `[hidden]` rule wins over the component's display. Undo brings it
   back exactly as before. The edge hides when a connected card is gone, as designed in P0-37.
3. **Delete is visible for one object.** A single selection also shows the count bar ("1
   selected") with its delete button, centred above the frame like the multi-selection bar, and
   clamped into the visible canvas. Clicking it deletes the selected object. The Delete and
   Backspace keys and the context menu keep working.

Behaviour that does not change: click to select, Escape to clear, resize, snap, band selection,
teammates, undo.

**Why:** a selection the owner cannot see, and a delete that does not delete, break the demo's
basic promise.

**Satisfies:** D3

## Acceptance

A1 WHEN an object is clicked, THEN a 2 px accent frame shows 4 px outside it with a concentric
radius, and dragging an edge or a corner still resizes it.
A2 WHEN a selected object is deleted by key, menu or count-bar button, THEN it disappears from the
canvas, the status says so, and Cmd/Ctrl+Z restores it.
A3 WHEN exactly one object is selected, THEN the count bar shows "1 selected" with a delete button
above it; WHEN several are selected, THEN the bar behaves as before.
A4 WHEN the gates run, THEN `board-check`, `design-check`, `changelog-entry` pass with records,
language-check is OK, en/de keys are identical, masthead P0-48, screenshots and log are committed.
