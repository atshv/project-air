# P0-32 — Zone titles: named, editable zone headers in the live demo

> UI interaction in the static design preview (`apps/web/preview/`). Owner note (2026-10-27): the
> live preview is not bound to the usual brief word or token budgets.

## Goal

Zones carry a name. Every zone in the live demo shows its title in a header at the top of its
frame; the title can be renamed inline, and the zone content starts below the header.

**Why:** owner request: zones can be given a title or name.

**Satisfies:** D3

## Scope

- `preview.js` — header geometry in the zone layout (header 40 px, content inset 40 + 16 px as in
  `docs/canvas-acceptance.md` L02), an editable title that follows the frame (no FLIP stretching),
  rename by click/typing, Enter or blur commits, Escape restores, empty falls back to the default,
  40-character limit, status announcement; a zone formed by grouping starts with the default "new
  zone" name; default names follow the locale switch, edited names stay.
- `preview.css` — header title styles (single line, ellipsis at rest, hover/focus affordance,
  ghost/emptying/collapsed states follow the frame, reduced motion).
- `index.html` — masthead `P0-32`, `?v=P0-32`; locales `en.json`/`de.json` (identical keys, du-form,
  `ss`); `README.md`; `.changes/P0-32-zone-titles.md` (web, minor).
- Evidence: screenshots (named zone, editing, renamed, grouped new zone, mobile) and
  `P0-32-preview.log`.

## Out of scope

- Persisting names, dragging a zone by its header, zone colours, engine or app code.

## Acceptance

A1 WHEN the demo loads, THEN the zone shows its name in a 40 px header and both items start
40 + 16 px below the frame top; the title is a single line with an ellipsis when too long.
A2 WHEN the title is clicked and edited, THEN Enter or blur commits (status announces the new name),
Escape restores the previous name, an empty name falls back to the default and the length is capped
at 40 characters; dragging items never starts from the title.
A3 WHEN two free items are grouped, THEN the new zone gets the default new-zone name; WHEN the
locale switches, THEN default names follow the language and edited names stay.
A4 WHEN reduced motion is on, THEN the title follows the frame without transition; the title fades
with ghost/emptying/collapsed frames; `board-check`, `design-check`, `changelog-entry` pass;
language-check OK; screenshots and log committed; masthead P0-32; en/de keys identical.
