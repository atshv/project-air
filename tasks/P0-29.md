# P0-29 — Zone collapse, re-grouping and smaller zoom controls

> **Size:** 400–900 words. UI interaction in the static design preview (`apps/web/preview/`).

## Goal

1. When **both** zone items (notepad and photo window) are pulled out, the empty zone **disappears
   with an animation** (it no longer stays as a blank panel).
2. Dragging the two **free/detached** items so they overlap makes them **group into a new zone**
   (both dock back into a freshly animated zone around them).
3. The detach/dock/zone motion is **smoother and more refined** than P0-28.
4. The zoom **+/− controls are smaller** (still accessible).

**Why:** the zone should vanish once empty, photo and notepad should re-form a zone when dragged
together, and the motion/zoom controls should be refined.

**Satisfies:** D3

hidden; with one item still inside the zone stays visible.

## Specifications

- Zone exit: opacity 1→0, scale 1→0.96 (short motion token), then hidden/inert; only when item
  count reaches 0. Zone enter: opacity 0→1, scale 0.96→1 while items ease to slots.
- Grouping runs during drag and on release; only free items group (docked behaves as P0-28);
  release mid-animation settles deterministically.
- Motion tokens only; reduced motion → instant. Group slots side by side (original spacing); zone
  bounds = union + 16 px padding, clamped to the surface. Zoom 32 px buttons in a ~36 px pill,
  glyph ~14 px, labels/focus unchanged.
- German only in `de.json`.

## Pitfalls

- Cancel/restart the zone exit when an item docks during it; never group two docked/animating
  items (state flag). Grouping must never add an edge or move endpoints.
- `design-check` rejects raw colours/off-grid spacing/numeric z-index; `language-check` German in
  code; `board-check` before any `pnpm`; `.changes` mtime = masthead time; keep P0-28 behaviour.

## Edge cases

- Both pulled out quickly → one clean exit; one pulled back during exit → zone re-enters/docks.
- Grouping at 0.5/1.5 zoom and 390 px: thresholds in screen px, slots clamped, no overflow.
- Release exactly at the threshold; two pointers; pointer cancel; locale switch mid-animation.
