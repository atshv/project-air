# P0-34 — Live demo: move a zone with its members

> UI interaction in the static design preview (`apps/web/preview/`). Owner note (2026-10-27): the
> live preview is not bound to the usual brief word or token budgets.

## Goal

A zone in the live demo ("Ausflug an den See" / "Lake trip", or any zone formed by grouping) can be
moved as a whole: pressing on its frame or its header and dragging moves the frame and every docked
member together, 1:1 with the pointer, without breaking any member interaction.

1. **Grip:** the frame's padding and its 40 px header (outside the name) start a zone drag after the
   4 px threshold; a member still drags only itself; a click on the name still renames.
2. **Movement:** frame, name and members follow the pointer without transition and with a lifted
   frame shadow; the frame stays inside the visible surface (below the toolbar on desktop), and the
   members keep their slots.
3. **Release:** the new place becomes the zone's committed origin; the status line announces
   "Zone moved." / „Zone verschoben.“. A `pointercancel` or a lost capture glides everything back.
4. **Robustness:** no zone drag while a member drag runs (and the other way round); resize and
   locale reflow during the drag are deferred; free items, the edge and the note cards never move;
   a collapsed zone is inert; reduced motion is instant.

**Why:** the owner wants to move the zone card itself, not only its items. ADR-0010 (zones as
containers) says that dragging a zone moves its content; the engine follows in P4-27 and P4-29, so
the preview shows the target behaviour.

**Satisfies:** D3

## Scope

- `preview.js` — a zone move gesture next to the member drag (shared threshold, pointer capture
  only after the threshold, clamping through the existing layout functions), deferred sync, the
  status announcement.
- `preview.css` — the frame becomes a grip (`cursor: grab`, `grabbing` while moving), a lifted
  shadow while moving, transitions off while moving; collapsed and ghost frames stay inert.
- `index.html` — masthead `P0-34`, `?v=P0-34`, the hint and legend mention the zone move.
- Locales `en.json`/`de.json` (identical keys, du-form, `ss`), `README.md`,
  `.changes/P0-34-zone-move.md` (web, minor), evidence screenshots and `P0-34-preview.log`.

## Out of scope

- Resizing a zone, moving free items with the zone, keyboard moving of a zone, persistence, engine
  work, new tokens or dependencies.

## Acceptance

A1 WHEN the zone is dragged by its frame or header, THEN the frame, the name and every docked member
move together 1:1, stay inside the visible surface and keep their slots; free items, note cards and
the edge do not move.
A2 WHEN the zone drag is released, THEN the new place is committed and announced; WHEN it is
cancelled, THEN frame and members glide back to where the drag started.
A3 WHEN a member is dragged or the name is clicked, THEN the member drag, the release preview and
the rename work exactly as before (P0-31, P0-32), and a zone drag never starts from them.
A4 WHEN the gates run, THEN `board-check`, `design-check`, `changelog-entry` pass with records,
language-check is OK, en/de keys are identical, masthead P0-34, screenshots and log are committed.

## Edge cases

- A press on the frame without movement does nothing (no rename, no jump).
- A zone dragged against the surface edge or under the toolbar stops at the clamp.
- A member dragged out right after a zone move uses the new slot positions.
- A zone formed by grouping moves the same way; a collapsed zone cannot be grabbed.
