# P0-31 — Refined live demo: smooth merge/split and calm motion

> UI interaction in the static design preview (`apps/web/preview/`). Owner note (2026-10-27): the
> live preview is not bound to the usual brief word or token budgets; read the whole preview.

## Goal

Rework the live demo as a whole so that pulling the zone items **apart** and **together** feels
smooth, predictable and calm:

1. **One motion language.** Every settle (dock, detach, reorder, group, collapse) uses one calm
   ease-out curve from the motion tokens, no overshoot/spring (design law §7), nothing jumps.
2. **Merge/split.** Detach shows a clear lift (shadow, slight scale) that ramps with distance;
   docking back and grouping glide from the release point to the slot; the zone grows/shrinks and
   appears/disappears around the items without animating layout properties.
3. **Feedback.** While dragging, the zone shows where the item will land (placeholder/drop hint),
   and the other docked item makes room live when a reorder would happen on release.
4. **Robustness.** No stuck classes, no flicker at the thresholds, second pointer/cancel safe,
   locale switch and resize mid-drag safe, reduced motion instant, keyboard cards unchanged.

**Why:** owner request to refine the whole live demo — merging/splitting and the animations.

**Satisfies:** D3

## Acceptance

A1 WHEN an item is dragged out of or into the zone, THEN it tracks the pointer 1:1, the lift ramps
smoothly, the zone shows the landing slot and the release settles with one calm ease-out curve
(no overshoot); the zone resizes without animating width/height.
A2 WHEN a docked item is dragged across the other one, THEN the other item makes room live and the
release settles both without a jump; orientation follows the placement with hysteresis.
A3 WHEN both items leave the zone, THEN it fades out and becomes inert; WHEN the two free items are
released onto each other, THEN a zone forms around them with a smooth glide.
A4 Reduced motion instant; cancel/second pointer/resize/locale switch mid-drag safe; no edge
created; `board-check`, `design-check`, `changelog-entry` pass; language-check OK; screenshots and
log committed; masthead P0-31; en/de keys identical.
