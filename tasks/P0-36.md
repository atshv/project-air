# P0-36 — Live demo: select and resize objects, photo without bar, edges behind

> Static design preview (`apps/web/preview/`). Owner note (2026-10-27): the live preview is not bound
> to the usual brief word or token budgets.

## Goal

Owner request (2026-10-28): "Cards, notes etc. can all be enlarged and lengthened freely. There is
no bar above photos; photos can be resized too, and when dragged into a card they take its size. The
connection lines run behind all cards."

1. **Selection chrome:** a click on a note card, the notepad, the photo, the checklist or the table
   card selects it; the selected object shows the engine's selection chrome — a 1 px `--accent`
   frame and eight 8 px `--accent` handles (corners and edge midpoints, L10/L14). A click on the
   empty canvas or `Esc` clears the selection. The frame follows the object while it moves.
2. **Resize:** dragging a handle resizes against the opposite edge or corner, 1:1 with the pointer,
   clamped to the canvas and to per-object minimums (cards 176 × 80 px as in the contract, notepad
   176 × 80, photo 128 × 96, blocks never below their content height — blocks grow instead of
   scrolling, L02). A docked member that is resized re-lays its zone; a resized note card redraws
   its edge.
3. **Photo:** no title bar; the image fills the window (`object-fit: cover`). It resizes freely
   when free; when it docks into a zone with another member it takes that member's size along the
   zone's flow (stacked: the same width, side by side: the same height), keeping its aspect ratio.
4. **Edges behind:** the connection line draws behind every object, zones included (owner decision;
   deviates from design law §8, where edges sit above zones — recorded in the README and as an open
   question for the design law).

**Why:** owner request, see above.

**Satisfies:** D3

## Acceptance

A1 WHEN an object is clicked, THEN it shows the accent frame with eight handles; a click on the empty
canvas or `Esc` clears it; dragging the object keeps the frame on it.
A2 WHEN a handle is dragged, THEN the object resizes against the opposite edge or corner, never below
its minimum or outside the canvas; a docked member re-lays its zone and a note card redraws its edge.
A3 WHEN the photo is shown, THEN it has no bar; WHEN it docks into a zone with another member, THEN it
takes that member's width (stacked) or height (side by side) with its aspect ratio.
A4 WHEN the edge is drawn, THEN it lies behind cards, blocks, zone items and zone frames; `board-check`,
`design-check`, `changelog-entry` pass, language-check OK, en/de keys identical, masthead P0-36,
screenshots and log committed.
