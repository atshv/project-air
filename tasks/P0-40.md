# P0-40 — Live demo: two teammates on the board

> Static design preview (`apps/web/preview/`). Owner note (2026-10-27): the live preview is not
> bound to the usual brief budgets.

## Goal

Owner request of 2026-10-28: "wenn möglich 2 weitere leute quasi als team arbeit darstellen, wie
sie im selben projekt dabei wären auf live demo". The live demo shows two simulated teammates,
**Lena** (`--tone-b`) and **Jonas** (`--tone-c`), working on the same board as the viewer. They
follow design law §12 and the presence model of P5-05 (cursor, selection, name, colour; ephemeral,
never stored):

1. **Remote cursors.** Each teammate has a 16 px pointer in their colour with a 12 px name label on
   a surface in that colour (dark text, `--bg`). The cursors sit on layer 7a
   (`--layer-remote-cursors`): above the objects and the snap guides, below the canvas chrome. They
   never take pointer input. The pointer is a CSS shape, not an icon: the ui-kit has no cursor
   icon, and its icon test fixes the set at ten.
2. **Working rhythm.** Each cursor follows a scripted, looping route over the demo objects. Lena
   goes from the checklist to the table card and to note A; Jonas goes from the photo to the zone
   header and to note B. Each leg glides for 600–1200 ms with an ease-out, without overshoot, using
   `transform` only. A dwell holds the cursor at the object. Positions are fractions of the target
   object's rendered box, so the cursors stay on their objects through drags, resizes, zone moves,
   zoom and window resizes. A target the viewer deleted is skipped.
3. **Remote selections.** While a teammate dwells on an object with a selection, that object shows
   a 1.5 px frame (`--border-width-strong`) in their colour, without a ring. The viewer's own
   selection keeps the §6 look and is never changed by a teammate. The teammates change no content,
   no history and no status line.
4. **Idle fade.** After 5 s without movement a cursor fades to opacity 0.4 (`--motion-fade`). It
   comes back to 1 when it moves again. Each loop has one long dwell that shows this.
5. **Presence line.** Above the canvas, outside the canvas chrome (§9 allows no new floating
   element), one quiet 12 px line reads "On this board: You, Lena, Jonas". Each name has an 8 px
   dot in its colour, and the line has an accessible description.
6. **Calm and cheap.** The animation runs only while the demo is on screen and the page is visible
   (IntersectionObserver, `visibilitychange`). Under reduced motion the cursors jump between
   positions without gliding or fading transitions. Nothing overflows at 390 px.

Texts (names, presence line, legend, hint sentence) live in `en.json` and `de.json` (identical keys,
du-form, `ss`). Section 12's §12 card points to the demo.

**Why:** the owner judges the product by the live demo; realtime presence (stage 5) is part of the
product promise and should be visible next to the single-person interactions.

**Satisfies:** D3

## Acceptance

A1 WHEN the demo is on screen, THEN two cursors with name labels in `--tone-b` and `--tone-c`
glide between demo objects on layer 7a, below the toolbar, and never block a click.
A2 WHEN a teammate dwells with a selection, THEN that object shows a 1.5 px frame in the person's
colour without a ring; WHEN a cursor rests for 5 s, THEN it fades to 0.4.
A3 WHEN the viewer drags, zooms or resizes, THEN the cursors and frames stay on their objects, and
the presence line names You, Lena and Jonas in EN and DE.
A4 WHEN the gates run, THEN `board-check`, `design-check`, `changelog-entry` pass with records,
language-check is OK, en/de keys are identical, masthead P0-40, screenshots and log are committed.
