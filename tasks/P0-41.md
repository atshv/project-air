# P0-41 — Live demo: rounded frames, darker done items, contents instead of text

> Static design preview (`apps/web/preview/`). Owner note (2026-10-27): the live preview is not
> bound to the usual brief budgets.

## Goal

Owner feedback of 2026-10-28 on P0-39/P0-40: "die kanten runden und die abgewählten texte noch
dünkler, so dass man sieht, dass es abgewählt ist … nimm unnötigen text weg … mache dort
inhaltsverzeichnis für die einzelnen punkte".

1. **Rounded frames.** The selection frame follows the corner radius of its object instead of
   drawing a sharp rectangle around a rounded card: `--radius-md` (14) for note cards and zone
   items, `--radius-lg` (20) for blocks and zones. The script reads the object's computed radius
   into `--frame-radius`. The same applies to the multi-selection frames (P0-37) and to the
   teammates' frames (P0-40), which are scaled with the zoom. The corner hover bracket becomes an
   arc that follows that radius, 2 px wide, with its arms reaching 8 px past the curve. The section
   10 specimen and L14 in `docs/canvas-acceptance.md` describe the rounded frame.
2. **Darker done entries.** A ticked checklist entry is clearly darker than an open one: text
   `--muted` at opacity 0.6 (about 40 % white on the dark canvas, against 92 % for open entries).
   The tick stays full `--accent`. Design law §6's "Done (checklist entry)" row states the value.
3. **Contents instead of text.** The demo section loses its three long blocks: the intro
   paragraph, the hint paragraph and the "·" legend. In their place:
   - one short intro line, "Try everything directly on the canvas. Interactive demo — not the
     engine; no data, no server.";
   - a compact **contents list** of the individual points. Each point is a native
     `<details>`/`<summary>` with a short title and one or two short sentences: move and keys;
     connection; dock, reorder and pull out; new zone by grouping; name and move a zone; select
     and resize; snap, band, menu, delete and undo; checklist and table; teammates; preview-only
     toolbar and zoom.
   The list is numbered, closed by default, keyboard operable (Enter/Space on the summary) and
   laid out in two columns on desktop and one on mobile. The hint id that the demo's
   `aria-describedby` uses stays on the list.

All texts live in `en.json` and `de.json` (identical keys, du-form, `ss`). The old intro, hint and
legend keys are removed.

**Why:** a sharp frame around a rounded card looks unfinished, a done entry must read as done at a
glance, and walls of text hide the demo.

**Satisfies:** D3

## Acceptance

A1 WHEN an object is selected, multi-selected or selected by a teammate, THEN the frame's corner
radius equals the object's radius at the current zoom, and the hovered corner shows an arc bracket.
A2 WHEN a checklist entry is ticked, THEN its text is `--muted` at opacity 0.6, visibly darker than
the open entries, without strikethrough.
A3 WHEN the demo section is opened, THEN only the short intro and the numbered contents list are
shown above the canvas; each point opens by pointer and keyboard in EN and DE, and nothing overflows
at 390 px.
A4 WHEN the gates run, THEN `board-check`, `design-check`, `changelog-entry` pass with records,
language-check is OK, en/de keys are identical, masthead P0-41, screenshots and log are committed.
