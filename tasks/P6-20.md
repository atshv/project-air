# P6-20 — Zone lifecycle and names on the web canvas (L08, part 2)

## Goal

Zones in `apps/web` live and die like in the live demo: two free objects dropped together form a
new zone with the default name, a zone that loses its last member is removed, every zone name can
be renamed inline in its 40 px header, the zone tool creates an empty zone, and deleting a zone asks
whether to keep or delete its content. All of it is saved and synced.

**Why:** owner decisions 2026-10-27 and 2026-10-29 (the app must behave like the live demo; close
the stage-7 gaps first). The engine has this since P4-30 (and `updateZone` since P6-14).

**Satisfies:** D12

## Scope

- `apps/web/` grouping: the engine zone-lifecycle ghost frame and landing slot during the drag,
  one `zone-group` operation on release; when the moved object is the only member of an existing
  zone, that zone wraps both and keeps its name.
- Emptied zone: dashed and dimmed while the last member leaves, removed on release as one undoable
  operation whose inverse restores name, slot and members.
- Names: rendered in the header (P6-16 binding); inline rename through the P4-23 overlay (click
  selects all, Enter/blur commits, Escape restores, whitespace collapsed, 40 characters, empty ->
  language default from messages "New zone" / "Neue Zone"); one `updateZone` per rename.
- Zone tool (L00) creates an empty zone by the L20 rule; delete asks keep/delete content in a dialog
  (contract delete policy), keyboard operable.
- Realtime: two clients grouping the same objects at once — define and test the outcome (no silent
  duplicate zone).
- Tests: pure tests; e2e for grouping, empty-zone removal, rename (incl. Escape), tool, delete with
  both policies, undo of each.
- Side-by-side screenshots app vs demo for grouping ghost, emptied zone and name edit at both
  viewports.
- `.changes/P6-20-<short-name>.md` (area `web`, kind `minor`).

## Out of scope

- Arrangement and dock preview (P6-19), image windows, nested zones, touch.

## Acceptance

A1 WHEN two free objects are dropped onto each other, THEN a new zone with the default name wraps
them in one operation; WHEN a zone loses its last member, THEN it is removed; one undo reverts each.
A2 WHEN a zone is renamed, THEN one `updateZone` is committed, a second client sees the name, and
Escape restores the old name with no operation.
A3 WHEN the task is done, THEN the screenshots are committed and `board-check`, `boundaries`,
`unit`, `typecheck`, `design-check`, `language-check` and `e2e` exit 0 with records; the change
entry exists.
