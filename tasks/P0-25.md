# P0-25 — Grouped zoom control and invisible anchors

## Goal

The demo's zoom control is one vertical grouped pill (plus, minus, fit) like the owner's reference,
and the connection anchors are invisible: the curved edge still snaps to the four side-midpoint
anchor positions, but no white dots are rendered.

**Why:** the owner wants the zoom control visually grouped and the anchor dots transparent; the
interaction stays the same.

**Satisfies:** D3

## Scope

- `apps/web/preview/` (all files):
  1. **Zoom control:** replace the three separate square buttons with one floating vertical pill
     at the bottom-left of the demo surface (12 px from the edge, canvas-chrome position per design
     rule §9). The pill contains three stacked 36-px segments (`+`, `−`, fit) inside a container
     with `--radius-md`/`--radius-lg`, `--surface` background, `--border` outline and 1-px
     separators (`--border`), 4-px padding; CSS-drawn glyphs only (no Unicode, no new ui-kit
     icons); hover, pressed, focus-visible and disabled states from the design rules; English
     `aria-label`s and the existing tooltip pattern.
  2. **Invisible anchors:** remove the visible white anchor dots (no `--fg` fill, no ring, no
     opacity-faked dots). Keep four anchor positions per card at the side midpoints as geometry
     only (top/right/bottom/left); the edge still chooses the nearest anchor pair and renders the
     curved `--tone-d` 1,5-px path between those positions. No DOM anchors, no decorative dots,
     nothing to see at rest.
  3. Keep zoom behaviour (0.5–1.5, 10 % steps, disabled at limits, fit shows both cards after
     dragging), drag threshold/pointer capture/clamping, keyboard 16 px, `Home`, reduced motion,
     the curve, mobile 390×844 and the dated meta line intact.
  4. Update the demo hint and `apps/web/preview/README.md`: anchors are invisible geometry, the
     control is grouped and preview-only, product zoom uses ui-kit icons later.
  5. Meta line: `Last change: P0-25 — Grouped zoom control and invisible anchors` plus the current
     date/time (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`, Europe/Zurich).
- `.changes/P0-25-zoom-pill.md` (area `web`, kind `patch`, one English line).
- Evidence: refreshed `reviews/evidence/P0-25-demo.png` (1440×900: vertical grouped control, no
  anchor dots, curved edge) and `reviews/evidence/P0-25-preview.log` with serve/screenshot and
  probe (zoom limits, fit after drag, no visible anchor elements, edge endpoints on the invisible
  anchor positions); keep older screenshots.

## Specifications

- **Control geometry:** container width 44 px, three 36-px segments, 4-px padding; radius
  `--radius-md`; background `--surface`; border `--border-width` solid `--border`; separators 1 px
  `--border`; buttons use `--fg` glyphs, `--faint` hover, focus outline per §6, disabled opacity
  0.4.
- **Glyphs:** plus = two centered 1.5-px lines; minus = one line; fit = four CSS corner brackets
  (2-px lines); sizes 16–20 px; no text characters.
- **Invisible anchors:** compute the four side-midpoint positions from each card's rect in world
  coordinates; choose the nearest pair; do not create anchor DOM elements or visible styling.
- **Edge:** unchanged curved Bézier path, `--tone-d`, 1.5 px, `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`.
- **Meta line and README** updated in the same task.

## Pitfalls

- Do not keep hidden `.demo-anchor` elements with a visible background; geometry only.
- Do not break drag hits: the control must sit above the world layer and only capture its buttons.
- Do not regress zoom/drag maths or the curve when removing anchors.
- Do not use `+`/`−`/`[ ]` characters as visible icons (CSS shapes only).
- Run `board-check` before any `pnpm install`.

## Edge cases

- Cards overlap → nearest anchor pair still deterministic; the curve keeps a minimum visible length.
- Fit after dragging → both cards visible; zoom buttons disabled at 0.5/1.5.
- Mobile: the pill stays inside the surface and does not cover a card’s drag area unnecessarily.
