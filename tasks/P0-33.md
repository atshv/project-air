# P0-33 — Live demo: checklist block and spreadsheet table card

> UI interaction in the static design preview (`apps/web/preview/`). Owner note (2026-10-27): the
> live preview is not bound to the usual brief word or token budgets.

## Goal

The live demo shows two more canvas objects next to the notes and the zone:

1. A **checklist block** with five example items that can be checked and unchecked, a done counter
   in its header ("2/5") and a status announcement.
2. A **card with a spreadsheet-style table**: column letters, row numbers, a header row, four data
   rows and a total row whose formula sums the totals; a formula bar shows the selected cell's
   reference and its value or formula; clicking a cell selects it.

Both are draggable like the other canvas objects (1:1 pointer, clamped to the canvas, the last
touched object on top of its layer) and sit on the blocks layer (design law §8).

**Why:** owner request: a checklist with five toggleable examples and an Excel-style table inside a
card, visible in the live demo.

**Satisfies:** D3

## Scope

- `index.html` — the two objects in the demo world, real checkboxes with labels, a semantic table
  (`<table>`, `<th scope>`), masthead `P0-33`, `?v=P0-33`.
- `preview.css` — block and table styles from tokens only (spacing grid, radii, borders, muted
  grid lines, tabular numbers), focus and hover states, compact layout, reduced motion.
- `preview.js` — generic draggable blocks (home positions as shares of the world, rescaled on
  resize), checklist counter and announcements, cell selection with the formula bar; dragging never
  starts on a checkbox or label, and a click without movement selects a cell.
- Locales `en.json`/`de.json` (identical keys, du-form, `ss`), `README.md`, `.changes/P0-33-*.md`
  (web, minor), evidence screenshots and `P0-33-preview.log`.

## Out of scope

- Editing cell values, formulas beyond the shown sum, persistence, engine work, new tokens or
  dependencies.

## Acceptance

A1 WHEN an item of the checklist is clicked or toggled with Space, THEN its checkbox flips, the
counter shows done/total and the status line announces it; five items exist, two are done at start.
A2 WHEN a table cell is clicked, THEN it is selected and the formula bar shows its reference and its
value, the total cell shows `=SUM(D2:D5)` (`=SUMME(D2:D5)` in German) and the sum matches the rows.
A3 WHEN the checklist or the table card is dragged, THEN it follows the pointer 1:1, stays inside the
canvas, keeps its relative place on resize and never starts a drag from a checkbox or label.
A4 WHEN the gates run, THEN `board-check`, `design-check`, `changelog-entry` pass with records,
language-check is OK, en/de keys are identical, masthead P0-33, screenshots and log are committed.
