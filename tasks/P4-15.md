# P4-15 — Snapping (L07)

## Goal

Dragging snaps live to neighbour and zone edges and centres within 6 px screen tolerance, draws at
most three 1 px `--accent` guides continuous across both elements plus 16 px, prefers a line over the
16 px grid, frees the position with `Shift`, and stores the snapped release as exactly one move
operation with inverse; a C++ suite covers edge, centre, tolerance and precedence, and `perf` stays
within basis + 10 %.

**Why:** L07 shapes every move, so its geometry must be pure and tested and its cost proven against
the L01 basis.

**Satisfies:** D10

## Scope

- `packages/engine/` snapping core (native, pure): from the dragged rectangle, document, spatial
  index, camera and viewport return the snapped world position plus up to three guides, or the
  unchanged position when nothing is in tolerance. Candidates are the left/centre/right and
  top/middle/bottom edges of live visible neighbours and zones (never the dragged object), within
  `tokens::snap_tolerance` screen px (world = tolerance / zoom). A line match beats
  `Camera::snapToGrid`; without a match the 16 px grid applies; `Shift` bypasses both. Candidate order
  is deterministic, the spatial index is reused, and nothing runs during pan or zoom.
- `packages/engine/` guides: the chrome pass draws the returned lines as 1 px `tokens::accent` quads,
  continuous across the dragged and the matched element plus 16 px, at most three; they are
  presentation state, never command-buffer instances and never document state, so object counts, hit
  tests and existing glyph/chrome assertions stay unchanged.
- Bridge/input: the P4-14 input layer requests a snap on every drag move with the modifier state,
  applies it to the preview and commits the snapped position through the P4-05 seam on release; the
  bridge exposes the request and the guides read-only.
- `apps/engine-playground/`: dragging a card, block or zone shows live guides and the snapped
  position, `Shift` frees the drag, and a deterministic capture (screenshot plus JSON record) under
  `reviews/evidence/` proves guides and snapped release at 1440×900 and 390×844.
- Tests: C++ tests for edge, centre, tolerance just below/at/above 6 px screen, precedence, grid
  fallback, `Shift` free, at most three guides, dragged-object exclusion, determinism and no work
  without a drag; an input-layer test that the committed position equals the snapped position.
  `.changes/P4-15-<short-name>.md` (area `engine`, kind `minor`); `docs/engine.md` documents
  snapping and guide chrome.

## Acceptance

A1 WHEN a dragged edge or centre is within 6 px (screen) of a neighbour or zone edge/centre, THEN the
position snaps to that line live, a line hit beats the grid, and the release stores the snapped
position as one move operation with its inverse; C++ tests cover edge, centre, tolerance and
precedence.
A2 WHEN no candidate is in tolerance, THEN the 16 px grid applies and the released position is a
multiple of 16; with `Shift` held the position is free, no guide is drawn, and the released position
is whatever the pointer produced.
A3 WHEN snapped, THEN at most three 1 px `--accent` guides are drawn continuous across both elements
plus 16 px, nothing runs during pan/zoom, object counts and reference images stay unchanged, and the
playground capture record proves the guide state.
A4 WHEN the task is done, THEN `board-check`, `boundaries`, `cpp-unit` and `perf` exit 0 with records
under `reviews/evidence/`, the measurement is within basis + 10 %, and `docs/engine.md` documents the
snapping pipeline.

## Specifications

- Values only from tokens: 6 px tolerance, 16 px grid, 1 px `--accent`, 16 px extension; no raw
  values (design-check).
- Tolerance is screen px, results world px, and nothing snaps while the camera changes.
- At most three guides in deterministic order, no hot-path allocation; guides never enter the
  document, the operation payload or the command-buffer counts.
- A snapped release is one operation (AD-1); a sub-4 px click stays a no-op (P4-14).
- `perf` is exclusive; never run it next to a browser gate.

## Pitfalls

- A grid snap after a line match would silently drop the line; precedence is one decision point.
- Snapping the dragged object to its own edges produces a stuck drag; exclude its slot.
- The wrong zoom divisor breaks the tolerance at 200 %; test several zoom levels.
- Guides as command-buffer instances change instance counts and drift the `visual` gate; keep them in
  the chrome pass.
- Rebuild bridge/WASM before the capture; run `board-check` before any `pnpm` command.
