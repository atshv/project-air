## Verdict

The playground switches backends with automatic fallback and a truthful HUD, the WebGPU path now
renders the full committed content (grid, objects, chrome and glyph/text) behind the same command
buffers and tokens, and the exclusive visual gate captures 56 frames — 28/28 per backend against its
own committed set, adapter positively identified, WebGL2 images unchanged. The cross-backend
comparison is recorded as content parity within a reviewed tolerance (≤ 3 % differing canvas pixels,
0 interior differing pixels, max 2.67 %, worst channel delta 201 on antialiased boundaries), not as
pixel identity. Lead re-ran the gate: pass.
