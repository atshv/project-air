## Verdict

The WebGPU module consumes the existing 24-byte command buffer and renders it with one instanced
WGSL draw on a real adapter (verified frame: adapter `apple`/`metal-3`, pipeline/ready/frame 1,
8 visible instances, committed screenshot shows the layers scene); the native stubs keep the suites
green, the WASM stays at 61,088 B gzip, and the WebGL2 path is byte-identical (lead re-ran the full
`visual` gate: 28/28 captures 0-diff). The only finding is a documented duplication introduced to
repair a pre-existing broken TS import.
