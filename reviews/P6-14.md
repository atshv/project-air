## Verdict

The engine speaks contract 0.5.0. The C++ static assert and the native tests are at 0.5.0, so the
wasm builds again on `main` (since the P6-12 merge the assert stopped any rebuild). The document port
has `rename` and `setContent`; `updateZone` and `updateBlockContent` are applied (kind checked,
empty content removes the field, content copied deeply). A zone rename is one `updateZone` with the
previous title as inverse, replacing the P4-30 group. A `block-content` gesture builds one
`updateBlockContent` with the previous (or empty) content as inverse. `blockContentPreview` and
`fitBlockToContent` let a block grow to its content through the L02 binding. `src/bridge/app.ts` is
the one bridge surface an app imports, and `docs/engine.md` explains what the app wires.
