## Open questions (worker)

- **(a) Divider 1 × 20 px with `--space-4` margins instead of the brief's `--space-24`:** accepted.
  The brief's own pixel value is 20 px, `--space-24` is 24 px and would contradict it; `--icon-bar`
  is the existing 20 px token, and the 1 px width and 4 px margins are tokenised. README:122-123
  documents the choice. No fix required, but the brief should be corrected if `--space-24` was the
  intent.
- **(b) Toolbar wrap at ≤520 px + world-layer offset:** not acceptable as implemented; see F1. The
  offset is needed, but the breakpoint must cover the whole collision range, and the actual wrap
  starts at ≤480 px, not ≤520.
- **(c) Image window 240 × 140 vs the brief's ~220 × 150:** accepted. It is on the 4 px grid, keeps
  roughly the same area, and aligns with the 240 px notepad; README:135 documents it. The owner may
  notice it is a little wider and shorter than the brief's approximation; a later trim is optional.
