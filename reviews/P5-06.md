## Verdict

The sync service now proves the stage's durability invariant: 20 acknowledged batches at contiguous
`serverSeq` 2…21 survive SIGTERM + restart, the reload (snapshot + replay) ends at `serverSeq` 21
with every acked operation present, catch-up from `fromServerSeq` is exact for empty/one/large gaps,
and compaction writes a snapshot at its sequence then prunes only covered updates (20 pruned,
snapshots `[1, 21]`), reproducing the uncompacted state after a restart. Graceful shutdown sets the
stopping flag before closing (no ack after shutdown starts) and closes sockets with a retryable code.
Lead re-ran the sync suite: 14/14 pass.

  harness, `--test-concurrency=1` because port/DB are exclusive).
- **Crash ordering:** one transaction writes the snapshot at its sequence and prunes `<=` it; a crash
  between the two leaves the snapshot plus now-unnecessary rows, which replay-after-snapshot ignores —
  an acknowledged update can never be pruned before a committed snapshot covers it.
