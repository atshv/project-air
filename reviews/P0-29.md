## Open questions

- **Collapsed zone inert (the owner's reading):** the intended reading is reasonable and matches the
  owner's "the zone disappears"; the implementation is only partially inert (pointermove gate, not
  release). Fix the release path rather than accepting the pop-back; the status "Zone hidden — drag
  the two items together to form a new zone" is misleading while a drop over the old area restores
  it. If the team prefers the forgiving release, A1, the README, the log and the status string must
  all say so.
- **Side-by-side at 1440 / stacked at 390:** acceptable. 526 px fits the 1136 px surface; at 390 the
  horizontal choice would not fit (526 > 358), so vertical 274 × 275 is the right call and fits the
  358 px surface. A fresh 390 grouping measured 274 × 275 vertical; no page overflow.
- **Fade screenshot (WAAPI pause at 18 ms, hide timer stretched):** acceptable. The log
  (`P0-29-preview.log:83-93`) gives the real, unpaused 180 ms timeline (opacity 1.00 → 0.60 →
  0.32 → 0.16 → 0.00, scale 1.000 → 0.9839 → … → 0.9600) and states the capture method; the
  screenshot is supplementary evidence, not the proof. Minor observation: the shot's status line
  still reads "Image window detached" because the hide announcement is at 260 ms — not a defect.
- **OR grouping threshold (≥ 25 % of the smaller area or centres ≤ 48 screen px):** reasonable and
  documented; the code uses the OR exactly (`preview.js:824-825`), screen px for the centre rule, and
  the log's 50 %-zoom probe shows the centre rule catching a 10.6 % overlap at ≤ 48 px. No issue.
- **Grouped zone on resize:** not acceptable as-is (F2); the viewports in scope are 1440, 390 and the
  owner's 1426 × 583, and a resize is a normal preview action. Fix the orientation re-evaluation.
- **Grouped zone under the floating toolbar (F7):** minor; the P0-28 cards can already go under the
  toolbar, so the same layering rule applies. Clamp or document.
- **Zone size vs the pre-group union (F6):** minor; the settled state looks right, but the "union +
  16 px" wording is not what the code does at grouping time. Centre or reword.
