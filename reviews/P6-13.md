## Verdict

`packages/crdt`, `services/sync` and `packages/sdk` speak contract 0.5.0. Block content is stored
per entry (checklist point, lane, lane entry, table column, row, cell), keyed by the entry's UUIDv7
id with a fractional position for ordered entries; `updateBlockContent` writes only the delta
between the embedded previous content (its inverse) and the new content, in both adapters
(`block-content.ts`, shared). `updateZone` renames a zone. A kind that does not match the block, a
missing block or zone and invalid content are rejected. Sync and SDK negotiate 0.5.0; stored 0.4.0
snapshots load as 0.5.0. The live socket test (real Yjs core) and the restart harness carry both
operations through ack, log, broadcast, restart, compaction and a fresh join.
