## Verdict

Wheel and pointer input is normalised in the input layer: two-finger scroll pans 1:1 without zoom,
`Cmd/Ctrl`+wheel zooms around the pointer with a stable anchor inside 0.5 world px and clamped at
0.1 … 8, a two-pointer pinch zooms around the midpoint, suppressed targets keep their own wheel and
an active object drag never moves the view. No second camera path, no rendering change and no
reference-image delta. The capture uses in-page synthetic events because CDP wheel dispatch hangs in
this headless build; that limitation is recorded, not hidden.
