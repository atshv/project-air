## Acceptance

### A1 — pass

- **Pure helper, exact rule.** `Camera::fitBounds(bounds, viewportWidth, viewportHeight, ViewFit&)`
  (`packages/engine/src/camera.cpp:81-116`) rejects a non-positive/non-finite viewport before touching
  the result, takes the L02 padding from `Camera::fitTopPadding()` (tokens `space_48 + space_16`,
  `:76-79`), computes `min(viewportWidth / bounds.width, (viewportHeight − 64) / bounds.height, 1.0)`,
  clamps to `[kMinZoom, kMaxFitZoom] = [0.1, 1.0]` and centres the bounds horizontally and in the
  area below the padding (`panX = bounds.x − contentScreenLeft / zoom`,
  `panY = bounds.y − contentScreenTop / zoom`). Empty, zero-area, negative and non-finite bounds take
  an explicit branch that frames the world origin at 100 % (pan 0/0, zoom 1), so there is no
  division by zero and no NaN pan. Nothing is stateful; the same inputs give the same output.
- **Exposed end to end.** `engine_fit_view` in the C ABI (`packages/engine/src/engine.cpp:28-45`,
  declared in `packages/engine/include/engine/engine.h:23-25`, exported in
  `packages/engine/CMakeLists.txt:51-52`), `Engine.fitView(bounds, viewport)` in the bridge
  (`packages/engine/src/bridge/engine.ts:82,331-370`, allocating and freeing a 3×f64 buffer through
  `HEAPF64` and throwing on a `0` return), the playground fit action on `f` and
