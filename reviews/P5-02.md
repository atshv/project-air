## Verdict

`packages/crdt` exposes only its own `BoardDoc` interface (`apply`, `encodeUpdate`, `applyUpdate`,
`snapshot`, `stateVector`, `encodeState`, `merge`); Yjs 13.6.27 (MIT, pinned) sits behind the package
boundary and a second fake adapter passes the same conformance suite. The seeded suite runs 10,000
randomized rounds to identical encoded state/snapshot for both adapters (Yjs ~99 ms, ~16 MB heap),
offline merges converge, snapshot round-trips and every operation+inverse restores a byte-identical
state. Lead re-ran the crdt suite: 8/8 pass; no Yjs import exists outside `packages/crdt`.
