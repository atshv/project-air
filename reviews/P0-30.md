## Open questions

- **Fit fallback swapping the requested orientation at 150 %:** acceptable as a keep-it-below-the-
  toolbar/inside-the-world rule; the requested orientation is retained and restored on a later
  resize. The owner may notice that a vertical drop becomes side-by-side while zoomed in; the status
  does say the actual (side-by-side) result. The horizontal clipping in F3 is the remaining rough
  edge, not the swap itself. Consider telling the user in the status that the zone was fitted, or
  clamping to the visible surface.
- **Reorder thresholds (dominance 1.2, 8 px order hysteresis):** reasonable. I measured a 4 px drop
  keeping `[notepad, window]` and a 12 px drop swapping; the 1.2 diagonal band keeps the current
  orientation on a near-diagonal drop, so it cannot flap. The 8 px band is a sane dead zone.
- **Drop-hint strength (subtle outline):** acceptable. It is a 1.5 px `--border-strong` outline with
  `outline-offset: var(--space-4)`, no layout change; visible on the target item in the live
  mid-drag and in `P0-30-drop-hint.png` without looking like a second selection state.
- **Detach commits on release:** matches the brief and the commit-on-release model. The detach look
  (progress 1, shadow, 1.03 scale) is visible during the drag but the state stays `docked`; the
  collapse starts only after the last release, so the zone does not vanish under the pointer. This
  is the correct reading of "detach is visual during the drag and commits on release" plus the P0-29
  collapse.
- **28 px painted in a 32 px hit area counts as "hit area ≥32":** yes. The button box is 32 × 32
  (the measured hit area), the `::before` inset of `--space-4 / 2` paints 28 × 28, and labels,
  tooltips and the focus ring are unchanged. The design-rules §3 coarse-pointer 44 px target is not
  used, but that predates P0-30 (P0-29 was also 32 px) and is outside this task's brief.
