## Acceptance

### A1 — pen draws over the whole board in world coordinates: pass

**Button, icon and mode.** `index.html:234–240` adds an eleventh toolbar button with one inline
SVG line icon in the §5 style (24 grid, `stroke-width="1.6"`, `currentColor`, round caps/joins,
`aria-hidden`, `focusable="false"`); `aria-label`/`data-label` come from the locale files. The
button inherits every toolbar state (rest, hover, focus-visible, pressed, `aria-pressed`) from the
existing `.demo-toolbar__button` rules. Clicking it sets `data-demo-pen="true"` on the surface,
selects nothing and updates the status line (`preview.js:2819–2831`); the whole world and every
descendant gets `cursor: crosshair` (`preview.css:1342–1350`, measured computed `crosshair` over
note A), and the world gets `touch-action: none`.

**One ink layer above all objects.** `index.html:448` adds exactly one `<svg class="demo-ink"
data-demo-ink>` inside `[data-demo-world]`, after the zone and the blocks. `preview.css:1248–1270`
puts it at `--layer-snap-guides` (`7`) with `pointer-events: none` and token strokes. Layer order
from `packages/ui-kit/tokens.css:85–90` is zones `3`, blocks `4`, note cards `5`, dragged/selected
`6`, snap guides/frame `7`, canvas chrome `8`; so ink is above zones, notes, photos (zone item),
table, checklist and any selected/dragged object, and below the toolbar, zoom pill, count bar and
undo hint. The selection frame, snap guides and band are also on `7`, but they are appended to the
world later than the static ink SVG, so they paint above the ink (verified
`frame.compareDocumentPosition(ink)` and equal computed `z-index: 7`); the teammate layer `7a`
(`preview.css:3575`) is a later sibling of the world and paints above it, as §8 requires.

**Gesture and smoothing.** Pointerdown starts one live path in the ink layer, pointermove appends
points, pointerup commits exactly one path (`preview.js:2758–2800`). The path is a smooth
quadratic polyline through the midpoints of the samples (`preview.js:2663–2687`), not a polygon.
A press with one point or under 2 world px of total movement is discarded (`INK_MIN_LENGTH`, lines
74–75 and 2737); samples closer than 1.5 world px are dropped. Independent headless run:
pointerdown + 15 moves + pointerup produced exactly one `path`, with a `M/Q/L` `d`; a 0.4 px
jitter produced no second path.

**World coordinates.** `inkPointOf` stores the inverse of the rendered world transform
(`preview.js:2652–2660`, `worldRatio` = rendered width / layout width, the same maths as the zone
drag at 1142–1149) and the SVG lives inside the transformed world, so pan/zoom cannot separate ink
from its content. Measured at 100 % → 150 %: note A went `176 × 122.8` at `232/21.3` → `264 × 184.1`
at `−12/−98.2`, stroke 1 `136 × 18.9` at `252/61.3` → `204 × 28.4` at `18/−38.2`; the stroke→note
relative offset stayed `0.11364 / 0.32582` (x/y) at both scales. The same invariance holds during
the 180 ms transform transition because the ratio is read from the rendered box.

**Drawing never selects or moves an object.** `onPenDown` is a capture-phase listener on the
surface and stops the press before any object or rubber-band handler sees it
(`preview.js:2758–2774`); entering pen mode also clears the current selection
(`preview.js:2819–2831`). Independent run: drawing across note A left its transform byte-identical
and the selection hidden; selecting note A and then choosing the pen cleared the frame without
dropping the ink; pointerdown on the ink layer itself passes through to the object below
(`pointer-events: none`).

**Exit, clipping, capture and jitter.** Escape first closes an open palette and then leaves the pen
(`preview.js:2802–2823`); any other tool leaves it via `onTool`; both verified, and Escape while a
stroke is live discarded the live path and left no ghost. The surface already has
`overflow: hidden` (`preview.css:1199–1212`), so the SVG's `overflow: visible` cannot spill onto the
page. Pointer capture is taken on the surface and pointerup/pointercancel are handled on the
document (`preview.js:2770, 2797–2798`); a stroke started on note A, moved outside the surface and
released outside committed exactly one path. The live stroke is cancelled on tool change/undo/redo
(`cancelInkStroke`).

### A2 — token palette, dot, keyboard and locales: pass

**Popover and dot.** The palette trigger keeps its toolbar button and gains a token-coloured dot
(`index.html:241–243`, `preview.css:1364–1375`); the popover has the six swatches
`--tone-a … --tone-e` and `--fg`, `role="group"`, a translated `aria-label`, `hidden` by default,
and the current swatch carries `aria-pressed="true"` (`index.html:245–252`). `applyColour` accepts
only `INK_TONES`, stores the **token name**, marks the swatch, sets `--ink-colour: var(--tone-x)` on
the demo and updates the dot (`preview.js:3489–3500`). Every stroke is rendered through
`stroke: var(--tone-x)` (`preview.css:1265–1270`), never a raw value.

**Theme recolour, measured.** In dark, a tone-a stroke computes `rgb(143, 191, 175)`; after the
light switch the same path computes `oklch(0.455 0.065 165)` while `data-ink-tone` stays
`tone-a` (tone-c likewise kept). The light palette is the preview-scoped block at
`preview.css:39–67`; both the light and dark tone tokens are real token overrides, not inline
colours.

**Keyboard and outside click.** `Enter` on the focused trigger opens the popover and focuses the
current swatch; `ArrowRight/Left/Up/Down` walk the swatches and wrap (`preview.js:3559–3571`);
`Enter` selects (`tone-c` verified), writes the dot (`rgb(184, 166, 224)`), closes the popover and
returns focus to the trigger, with status “Pen selected — Lilac. Draw on the board; Escape ends
the pen.”; `Escape` closes the popover, returns focus and keeps the pen active; a second Escape
leaves the pen. A press outside the popover (and a focus move out) closes it
(`preview.js:3573–3595`). The colour survives tool changes (`--ink-colour: var(--tone-c)` after
selecting the zone tool).

**Locales and raw values.** `en.json`/`de.json` have 533/533 identical keys, no `ß`, and the new
strings are in du-form (DE “Zeichne auf der Leinwand”). Added CSS/JS/HTML contains no hex, `rgb()`,
`hsl()` or `oklch()`; the palette uses `--space-*`, `--radius-*`, `--shadow`, `--border-*`,
`--outline-*` and `--motion-hover`, with a `prefers-reduced-motion` override.

### A3 — history, status, locale/theme: pass (caveat F2)

**One stroke = one translated step.** `recordChange(before, "draw")` is called once on a committed
stroke (`preview.js:2792`) and every snapshot now carries a deep copy of the strokes
(`preview.js:2478–2481, 2551–2557`); `undo`/`redo` restore the before/after snapshots and clear the
other stack (`preview.js:2575–2618`), and redo is bound to `Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+Z`
(`preview.js:3209–3222`). Independent run with two strokes in two colours: undo removed exactly one
(“Undo: Draw”), redo restored it (“Redo: Draw”) with the tones `[tone-a, tone-c]` intact, two undos
emptied the ink, two redos restored exactly the two strokes. In DE the hints read
“Rückgängig: Zeichnen” / “Wiederholen: Zeichnen”. A cancelled/aborted stroke adds no step.

**Existing snapshot history.** A card drag still records a step and undo restores the card’s
transform while keeping the strokes; redo re-applies the drag and keeps the ink. The resize/zone
handlers and `recordChange`’s signature are untouched; only the snapshot payload was extended.
The checklist was **already outside** the snapshot on the base commit (`git show
1034527:apps/web/preview/preview.js`), so checklist toggles were never undo steps; its toggle,
counter and status still work and P0-43 does not change that (documented in the notes).

**Status, theme/locale and reload.** The status line reports the pen and the current colour
(`demo.status.pen`, `demo.status.ink`, `demo.status.stroke` in both locales; measured “Stroke drawn
in Sage.” / “Pen selected — Lilac…”). Theme and language switches keep the strokes and their token
names; reload clears the ink (`paths 1 → 0`, `pen` attribute gone) while the stored locale/theme
remain — preview-only as documented.

**F2 caveat.** Pressing Cmd/Ctrl+Z *while still holding the pointer* cancels the live stroke and
undoes the previous step, but the release is then recorded as a second, spurious history step:
after undo of stroke 1 (0 paths, “Undo: Draw”), the next undo restored stroke 1 and said “Undo:
Move”; redo said “Redo: Move”. Root cause: `cancelInkStroke`/`finishInkStroke` clear the live ink
but not `pressSnapshot`, so the deferred surface pointerup recorder (`preview.js:2621–2630`)
records the undo itself; `onPenEnd` returns early because the stroke is already null
(`preview.js:2785–2792`). See F2.
