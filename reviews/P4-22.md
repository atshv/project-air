## Verdict

The playground persists the camera per board and device through a guarded, versioned record, restores
a valid view before the fitted frame, falls back to the L19 fit otherwise, and never shares a viewport
across boards. Round 1 shipped a broken page boot (a TDZ `markViewChanged` reference in the host
callbacks and a capture hook bug) and was returned; round 2 fixed both, disabled persistence in the
deterministic harness contexts (`store=off`, `view=`, `backend=`) after it had disturbed WebGPU
captures, and produced the missing 8/8 browser capture plus a 56/56 0-diff visual re-run.
