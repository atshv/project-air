   pointer fell outside and the zone re-anchored. Fixed with size-normalised offsets, the half-item
   overlap hold and decisions against drag-start positions.
2. The dragged notepad could pass under the free photo (same layer). The last touched item is now
   the selected one at `--layer-dragged-or-selected`.
3. Cards kept stale positions after a narrow → wide resize (card B stuck on the left). Cards, free
   items and the zone now rescale by the world size ratio.
4. The orientation announcement read the preview orientation; it now compares against the committed
   layout.
